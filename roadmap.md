# Roadmap

This outlines the progression of the argument.

## Part I — Necessary Foundation
- Establish existence
- Reject total dependency
- Conclude non-dependent reality

## Part II — Contingency of the Universe
- Show variation in constants and structure
- Argue universe is not necessary

## Part III — Rejection of Brute Fact
- Show brute facts fail as explanation

## Part IV — Possibility vs Necessity
- Are alternatives real or just conceivable?

## Part V — Nature of the Foundation
- Passive vs active
- Structure vs source

## Part VI — Toward Mind
- Does grounding require intelligence?

## Part VII — Conclusion
- From foundation to God
