# Proving God Existence in 1000 Lines

## Part I: The Necessity of a Non-Dependent Foundation


### 1. Existence is undeniable

1. Something exists.  
2. This is undeniable, because denial would itself require existence.  

### 2. Dependency and independence

3. What exists is either dependent or non-dependent.  
4. Dependent means it relies on something else to exist.  
5. Non-dependent means it exists by its own nature.  

### 3. Rejection of total dependency

6. Not everything can be dependent.  
7. If everything were dependent, then everything would rely on something else.  
8. A chain of only dependent things provides no ultimate explanation.  
9. Explanation cannot consist only of things that require explanation.  

### 4. Existence of a necessary foundation

10. Therefore, there must be at least one non-dependent reality.  
11. This non-dependent reality does not rely on anything else.  
12. It exists necessarily, not contingently.  

### 5. Necessary vs contingent

13. Contingent things are those that could have been otherwise.  
14. Necessary reality is that which cannot fail to exist.  

### 6. Contingency of the universe

15. The observable universe contains change, variation, and dependency.  
16. Things in the universe begin, end, and depend on conditions.  
17. Therefore, the observable universe is not necessary in its current form.  
18. It is contingent or contains contingent features.  

### 7. Need for grounding

19. Contingent reality does not explain its own existence.  
20. It requires a grounding that is not contingent.  
21. Therefore, the non-dependent reality grounds contingent reality.  

### 8. Nature of grounding

22. This grounding is not a temporal sequence.  
23. It is not “before” in time, but prior in explanation.  

### 9. Foundation

24. The non-dependent reality is the foundation of all existence.  
25. Any complete explanation of reality must include this foundation.  

### 10. Termination of explanation

26. A foundation cannot itself be contingent.  
27. Otherwise, it would require further grounding.  
28. Therefore, the chain of explanation terminates in a non-dependent foundation.  
29. This foundation exists by necessity, not by chance.  
30. This establishes the minimal requirement for any ultimate explanation of reality.
